How this tracker applies your rules

  1. Normal mode starts with 0.1% of the cycle-start bankroll. Each consecutive WIN increases the next bet by the payout multiplier. Complete 3 consecutive wins to start a fresh cycle at the current balance.
  2. A LOSS before the win target starts standard recovery. The recovery target stays fixed at the bankroll recorded at the beginning of that cycle. The next bet progresses by 1.31× after every result.
  3. If balance reaches or falls below 90% of the cycle-start bankroll, drawdown recovery activates. Its first bet is 1% of the balance at activation; after that, the next bet progresses by 1.31× after every result.
  4. Recovery ends when balance is back at or above the fixed cycle-start target. The tracker then starts a new normal cycle with a 0.1% base bet.
  5. A WIN adds (multiplier − 1) × bet to bankroll; a LOSS subtracts the full bet. Bet amounts are rounded to the configured display precision.
Important: this is a record-keeping calculator, not a winning system. A 1.31× progression does not guarantee recovery; a sequence of losses can exhaust the bankroll. Check Stake's minimum/maximum bet limits and actual payout/rounding before using any amount.
